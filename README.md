# Bloom Into You 🌸

> Some feelings begin before we know where they came from.

An unofficial, noncommercial fan website for *Bloom Into You* (*やがて君になる*): a small, personal place for the moments that stay after the story moves on. Available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese.

It discusses the complete manga, including the ending.

**[Visit the website →](https://jack11111eee.github.io/bloom-into-you/)**

## About the story

*Bloom Into You* is a manga by Nio Nakatani, published by KADOKAWA in eight volumes (2015–2019) and adapted into a TV anime by TROYCA in 2018.
